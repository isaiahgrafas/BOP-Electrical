What We Do

Every electrical job,
done right.

From a single power point to a full new build installation — BOP Electrical brings 20+ years of experience to every job. We carry common parts, work cleanly, and leave your property tidy. No drama, no mess, just great work.

Residential

New builds, renovations, rewires, switchboard upgrades, lighting, power points, heat pumps, EV chargers and more across the Bay of Plenty.

Commercial

Office and retail fit-outs, lighting design, data and communications, preventative maintenance plans and emergency lighting for businesses.

Rural

Comprehensive rural electrical services tailored to remote and off-grid areas. Sheds, farm buildings, pump installations and rural infrastructure.

24/7 Emergency

Electrical emergencies don't wait. Our call-out service is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for urgent faults, outages and safety issues.

Solar & EV Chargers

Solar system installation and EV charger setup for homes and businesses. Future-proof your property with clean energy solutions across the BOP.

Security & Data

CCTV, security alarms, access control, data and fibre optics, audio-visual systems and home automation. Complete connectivity solutions.

About Us

Locally owned.
Bay of Plenty proud.

Bay of Plenty Electrical is a family owned and operated company that has been serving the Bay since 2013. With 20+ years of combined experience, we handle jobs of any size — from a single power point to a full new build fit-out.

We're honest, we communicate clearly, and we turn up when we say we will. We carry common parts so we can get the job done in one visit, we use drop sheets, and we clean up when we leave.

We meet all legal and professional requirements for electricians in NZ — and we're happy to answer any questions along the way.
